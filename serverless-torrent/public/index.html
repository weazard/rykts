    Pieces are SHA-1 verified in the browser before they touch storage, so a slow or malicious peer can never corrupt your download. Peer discovery (/api/announce) is decoupled from downloading (/api/download) so each download call spends its whole budget transferring data, not rediscovering the swarm.
